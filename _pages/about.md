<strong>Hi, I'm Jess Hayden — Your Healthcare Data Consultant.</strong> 
I help small to mid-size healthcare organizations across the United States eliminate the headache of manual reporting. By building secure, automated data pipelines, I give your team their time back—allowing you to focus on analyzing data and making critical decisions rather than getting bogged down in creating the reports.

<strong>The Problem: Trapped in Spreadsheet Chaos</strong>
Every healthcare leader knows that clean data is vital for operations, compliance, and patient care. But without the budget for a full-time data team, internal staff often spend dozens of hours each week manually copying, pasting, and patching together reports.

This manual process doesn't just waste time; it introduces data quality risks and pulls your team away from their actual jobs.

<strong>How I Help: Secure, Budget-Friendly Automation</strong>
I bridge that gap. Based in New Jersey and serving clients nationwide, I build custom, <b>HIPAA-conscious</b> data solutions that automate your existing manual workflows remotely.

By leveraging powerful, open-source programming like <b>Python</b> alongside robust <b>SQL</b> databases, I create highly efficient reporting systems without forcing your organization into expensive, recurring Business Intelligence (BI) software licenses. You get enterprise-grade data reliability on a mid-size budget.
